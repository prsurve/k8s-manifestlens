ManifestLens

Kubernetes YAML in plain English, with risky settings flagged.

Runs in your browser. Your YAML is never uploaded.

Each line of your manifest, explained, with a link to the official docs.

Flags missing limits, root containers, unpinned images and more.

Paste the old and the new version to see what applying the change will do.

Add your own rules (Check and Diff)

Rules are plain YAML. See the rule guide.
